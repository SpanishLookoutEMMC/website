What you can expect

Gathered around Jesus. Planted in Spanish Lookout.

It doesn't matter what your heritage is, we are a multi-cultural family of believers that gathers around Jesus Christ.

We care about teaching the Scriptures faithfully, sharing one another's joys and burdens, and providing a place to worship and fellowship with other believers.

While you may hear some speak plautdietsch in casual conversation, our services are in English. With a mix of contemporary and traditional songs, together with clear sermons from the Scriptures, we trust the Spirit will grow us up together into Christ.

When we gather

Join us on Sunday mornings

Prayer: 9:00 am — Offer and receive the ministry of prayer
Coffee & Fellowship: 9:30 am
Worship Service: 10:00 am
During the sermon, children ages 3–11 are dismissed for Children's Church

How to find us

Spanish Lookout,
Cayo District, Belize.

Spanish Lookout is a Mennonite farming community in western Belize, about a two-hour drive from Belize City. We are located on Route 35 West, Spanish Lookout (near Duck Run 3).
