Lead Pastor

Blaine Dueck

Blaine serves as Lead Pastor of Spanish Lookout EMMC. He is passionate about teaching the Word of God in ways that bring clarity, encouragement and challenge. He does not shy away from addressing difficult topics but seeks to do so in a grace-filled, Christ-centered way. In his spare time, he enjoys playing board games with his family, disc golfing and crafting simple projects out of wood.

Blaine & his wife Melissa married in 2006 and have been pastoring at the Spanish Lookout EMMC since 2017.
