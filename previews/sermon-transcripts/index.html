We welcome you to worship with us.

A Mennonite congregation in the Cayo District of Belize. Each Sunday morning we gather to sing, to pray, and to open the scriptures together. Whether you live up the road or are passing through, we would love to welcome you.

We teach with care in English, and we try to live as people who encourage, equip, and send one another in the way of Jesus.
