News · April 20, 2026

Easter Sunday service · 10 AM

Our Easter service gathered the congregation around the good news of the resurrection, with baptisms to follow in the weeks ahead.

Easter morning the sanctuary was full — visitors, families, and those who have been walking with this church for years. We sang, we read from the gospels, and we heard the familiar and astonishing news: He is risen, indeed.

"He is not here; he has risen, just as he said." — Matthew 28:6

Pastor Blaine preached from Matthew 28 on what the resurrection meant for the disciples and what it continues to mean for the ordinary life of faith. The sermon is now up on our YouTube channel.

After the service, many stayed for coffee and conversation. In the coming weeks we will also be celebrating several baptisms — an echo of what this morning was really about.

If you visited us this Sunday: thank you for coming. Our door is open next week at 10 AM.
