A word from the church

We welcome you to worship with us.

Spanish Lookout · Cayo · Belize

Spanish Lookout EMMC is a Mennonite congregation in the Cayo District of Belize. Each Sunday morning we gather to sing, to pray, and to open the scriptures together. Whether you live up the road or are passing through, we would love to welcome you.

We teach with care in both English and Low German, and we try to live as people who encourage, equip, and send one another in the way of Jesus.

Our mission

Encourage, equip, and send.

Making disciples · Connecting churches
No. 01
Encourage.

Walking with one another through every ordinary and extraordinary season of life — in worship, fellowship, and celebration, in English and Low German.

John 13:34–35 · Hebrews 10:23–25
No. 02
Equip.

Opening the scriptures week by week so each believer may grow into maturity in Christ and be equipped for works of service.

Ephesians 4:11–16 · 2 Timothy 3:16–17
No. 03
Send.

Carrying the good news of Jesus into our work, our relationships, and our world — to Spanish Lookout and to the nations.
