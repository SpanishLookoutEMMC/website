Spring potluck & fellowship lunch

Tables stretched down the hall for our spring potluck — a simple afternoon of food, conversation, and life shared together.

Tables stretched down the hall and out into the foyer. Dishes came from every corner of the congregation — familiar recipes, new experiments, generous portions.

These meals are some of the simplest things the church does, and some of the most important. An hour of food and unhurried conversation does more than it looks like it does.

Thank you to everyone who cooked, set up, cleaned up, and stayed to talk.
