About us

Gathered around Jesus. Rooted in Spanish Lookout.

Established as a fellowship of EMMC

We are part of the Evangelical Mennonite Mission Conference — a family of churches across Canada, Belize, and beyond, committed to making disciples and connecting churches in the way of Jesus.

Here in Spanish Lookout, that looks like a steady Sunday rhythm: singing, prayer, reading of scripture, and a sermon opened to us by our pastor, Blaine Dueck. We care about teaching that is honest to the Bible, and we care about doing it in ways that serve our community — in both English and Low German.

We are not a large church. That is a gift. It means we know each other's names, we share in one another's joys and burdens, and we trust the Spirit to grow us up together into Christ.

When we gather

Sunday mornings,
ten o'clock.

Service begins at 10 AM and runs about ninety minutes. Coffee and fellowship follow. Children are welcome in the service; please ask a greeter about our kids' space.

How to find us

Spanish Lookout,
Cayo District, Belize.

Spanish Lookout is a Mennonite farming community in western Belize, about a two-hour drive from Belize City. The meeting house is easy to find from the main road.
