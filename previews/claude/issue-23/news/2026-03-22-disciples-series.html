New sermon series · "Disciples"

A new Sunday series exploring what it means to actually follow Jesus — a few weeks on prayer, service, and the ordinary life of discipleship.

We are a few weeks into a new Sunday series called Disciples — an attempt to take the ordinary language of following Jesus seriously.

The series is working through what discipleship actually looks like: how we pray, how we serve, how we relate to money and neighbour and enemy. Not as a program, but as a way of paying attention to the life we already have.

Sermons are posted to our YouTube channel each week if you miss a Sunday or want to share one.
