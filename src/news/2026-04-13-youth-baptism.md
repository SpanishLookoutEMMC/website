On a bright Sunday afternoon, seven young people from our congregation were baptized — a moment the church had been praying toward for some time.

Friends, parents, and the wider congregation gathered to witness each profession of faith. The service was simple: a few words, a question, an answer, and water.

We are grateful for each of these young people and for the years of teaching, friendship, and prayer that led to this day. Welcome to the fellowship of the church.
